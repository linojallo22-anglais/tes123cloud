TITLE
McDonald's Makes More From Rent Than Burgers: The Real Estate Empire Story

DESCRIPTION
In 2024, McDonald's collected $10 billion in rent from its franchisees, almost twice the $5.6 billion it made in royalties. This is the story of how a 52-year-old milkshake mixer salesman, two brothers from San Bernardino and a finance man named Harry Sonneborn turned a 15-cent hamburger into one of the biggest real estate businesses on Earth.

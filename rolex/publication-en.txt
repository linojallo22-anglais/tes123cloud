TITLE
Nobody Owns Rolex: How an Orphan Built a Watch Empire With No Owner

DESCRIPTION
Rolex sells an estimated 10 billion Swiss francs of watches a year, and nobody owns it. No shareholders, no stock price, no heirs. This is the story of Hans Wilsdorf, an orphan from Bavaria who bet everything on the wristwatch, proved it with a Channel swimmer and a trip to the bottom of the ocean, then gave his entire company to a foundation.

TITLE
Blockbuster Almost Killed Netflix. Then It Killed Itself.

DESCRIPTION
In 2000, Netflix offered to sell itself to Blockbuster for $50 million, and Blockbuster's CEO reportedly tried not to laugh. But the real story is stranger: Blockbuster saw the internet coming, signed a streaming deal with Enron, killed its own late fees, and by 2007 was actually beating Netflix. Then an activist billionaire walked into the boardroom. This is how the king of Friday night went from 9,094 stores to one.

TITLE
Enron: The $101 Billion Company That Only Existed on Paper

DESCRIPTION
In 2000, Enron reported almost $101 billion in revenue. Fortune called it America's most innovative company six years in a row. By the end of 2001, it was bankrupt. This is the story of mark-to-market accounting, secret partnerships named after Star Wars characters and dinosaurs, a CEO who called Enron's stock "an incredible bargain" weeks before it collapsed, and the paper shredder that took down one of the biggest accounting firms in the world.

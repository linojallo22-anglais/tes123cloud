TITLE
Every Time America Bought Land (And the Best Deal Ever)

VARIANTS
America Bought Half of Itself. Here's How
How America Went Shopping for a Country

DESCRIPTION
In 1803, the United States doubled in size for about three cents an acre. In 1867, it bought Alaska and people called it a folly. And in 1946, it secretly offered gold for Greenland. This is every time America bought land: Louisiana, Florida, the Mexican Cession, the Gadsden Purchase, Alaska, the Virgin Islands, the Philippines, the Panama Canal Zone, and the one that got away. Which deal was the best, and which one was the most expensive?

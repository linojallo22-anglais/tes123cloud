TITLE
The 28-Year-Old Who Sank a 233-Year-Old Bank

DESCRIPTION
Barings helped the United States buy Louisiana. A French statesman once ranked it next to Europe's great empires. Then, in 1995, a young trader in Singapore hid his losses in a secret account numbered 88888, made one giant bet on Japan's stock market, and watched an earthquake destroy it. Three days after he fled, leaving a note that said "I'm sorry", Britain's legendary bank collapsed. A few days later, it was sold for one pound.

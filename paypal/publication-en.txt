TITLE
PayPal: From "Worst Idea of 1999" to Turning Down $53 Billion

DESCRIPTION
In July 2026, Stripe and Advent offered more than $53 billion for PayPal, and PayPal's board said it was too low. Not bad for a startup that began by beaming money between PalmPilots. This is the story of Max Levchin, Peter Thiel and Elon Musk: a referral war that burned millions, a fraud crisis, a boardroom coup during a honeymoon flight, a $1.5 billion sale to eBay, and the "PayPal Mafia" that went on to shape Silicon Valley.
